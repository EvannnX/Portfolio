Robotic Arms in Art

2025

Created by
Andy Garcia, Taojie Zhang, Bang Xiao
Context
NYU Shanghai · Interactive Media Arts
Supported by
Franka Robotics

Three studies with the Franka Research 3 arm, made with Assistant Arts Professor Andy Garcia to investigate how industrial-grade robotics can support artistic practice. The work began with the off-the-shelf Desk tooling and moved to franky-control Python workflows for full access to the robot’s seven-axis kinematics.

Contemplation

The arm drags combs, stones and brushes through fine sand, erases the pattern, and begins again.

The Mimic Brush

A collaborative painter. With TensorFlow.js hand pose detection, visitors guide the brush by moving their hands.

Signals of the Void

Three speakers pulse audio cues. Reinforcement learning rewards the arm for moving toward louder signals, so its movement is a search driven by what it hears.
