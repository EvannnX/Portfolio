Spiral Genesis

2025

Context
NYU Shanghai · Interactive Media Arts
Materials
Arduino · Servos · 3D printing · Metal fabrication

Spiral Genesis is a kinetic sculpture that sets organic spiral forms in mechanical motion. It starts from spirals found in nature, such as nautilus shells and galaxy arms, and rebuilds that growth pattern as programmed movement: an Arduino program drives servos through choreographed sequences, with smooth transitions between spiral configurations. The structure is a modular build of 3D-printed and metal parts, shown as an exhibition piece.
