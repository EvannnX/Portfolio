2024-2025 VEX High Stakes

2024 to 2025

Team
NYU Shanghai Robotics Club
Role
Club founder and president, VEX team lead
Result
Second Prize

This is the NYU Shanghai Robotics Club's entry for the 8th China University Intelligent Robot Creative Competition (第八届中国高校智能机器人创意大赛), where the team won a Second Prize in the VEX U game High Stakes. As club founder and president, Bang Xiao led the team through robot design, autonomous programming, assembly, debugging and competition strategy.

The robot is built on the VEX V5 platform, with high-torque motors, encoders, a gyroscope and vision sensors, and a modular structure that can be changed quickly between matches. The team prepared separate strategies for the autonomous and the driver-controlled periods.
