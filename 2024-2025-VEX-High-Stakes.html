2024-2025 VEX High Stakes

NYU Shanghai Robotics Club at the 8th China University Intelligent Robot Creative Competition.

Context
NYU Shanghai · Robotics Club
Role
Club president · VEX team lead
Focus
VEX robotics · Competition · Engineering
Stack
VEX V5, RobotC, mechanical design
Year
2024-2025

Case

Compete in the 8th China University Intelligent Robot Creative Competition (第八届中国高校智能机器人创意大赛) with the NYU Shanghai Robotics Club.

Task

Design, build and program VEX robots for the High Stakes game, for both the autonomous and the driver-controlled periods.

The competition

The 8th China University Intelligent Robot Creative Competition (第八届中国高校智能机器人创意大赛) is a national robotics competition for university students. This year's VEX game, High Stakes, combines autonomous and driver-controlled tasks, so it tests both the mechanical design and the code.

The NYU Shanghai Robotics Club entered alongside universities from across China.

NYU Shanghai Robotics Club

The team draws students from engineering, computer science, mathematics and design.

What we worked on:

  • Autonomous programming and sensor integration
  • Mechanical design tuned for match performance
  • Team coordination and project management
  • Game analysis, and adapting the robot between matches

The robot

The robot is built on the VEX V5 platform. It was designed to handle several game elements and to stay reliable under competition pressure.

Hardware:

  • VEX V5 Brain for autonomous and driver control
  • High-torque motors for movement and manipulation
  • Encoders, a gyroscope and vision sensors
  • A modular build for quick changes between matches

Strategy

High Stakes asks robots to manipulate objects, navigate the field and hold positions. We prepared separate strategies for the autonomous and the driver-controlled periods.

We tested and revised the robot against different game scenarios until it ran consistently.

Team

The robot took months of work across the club, with members covering mechanical design, programming, strategy and project management.

What we learned:

  • Robot programming and control
  • Mechanical design and optimization
  • Building a competition strategy and adapting it
  • Running a project as a team
